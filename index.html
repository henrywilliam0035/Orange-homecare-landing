About Orange Homecare

WHY We Exist

We believe that everything can be improved upon.

We believe that transformation doesn't start with grand gestures or government policies — it starts at home. We believe that the ordinary Nigerian deserves extraordinary solutions. We believe that when you improve the smallest unit of society — the home — you begin to transform an entire nation.

Too many Nigerian families are forced to choose between quality and affordability, between what works and what they can afford, between what's best for their homes and what's best for their wallets. We believe this choice shouldn't exist.

That's why Orange Homecare exists — to improve the lifestyle of the ordinary Nigerian, one home at a time.

HOW We Do It

We challenge the status quo of the cleaning products industry in Nigeria.

While others create expensive premium products for the few or low-quality alternatives for the many, we create high-performance, eco-friendly solutions that are accessible to everyone. We do this through:

  • Innovation Without Compromise: Developing proprietary formulations that deliver premium results at everyday prices
  • Sustainable Design: Creating refillable systems that reduce waste while reducing costs
  • Direct Connection: Building relationships with our customers through accessible WhatsApp ordering and retail partnerships
  • Relentless Quality: Using safe, NAFDAC-certified ingredients that are tough on grease but gentle on hands and the environment

WHAT We Make

Gleam Dish Wash — Our flagship product is more than just a cleaning solution. It's proof that Nigerian households can have:

  • Powerful cleaning that cuts through the toughest grease
  • Eco-friendly, refillable packaging that protects our planet
  • Gentle, non-toxic ingredients safe for every family member
  • Premium quality at prices that respect your budget
  • NAFDAC certification for complete peace of mind

Our Founder

Henry founded Orange Homecare in 2025 with a mechanical engineer's mind and a visionary's heart. He saw that improving society didn't require waiting for systemic change — it required creating better solutions for everyday problems. Starting with something as simple as dish washing liquid, he set out to prove that ordinary Nigerians deserve — and can access — extraordinary quality.

Our Promise

Every bottle of Gleam Dish Wash is a commitment to you and to Nigeria. A commitment that says: your home matters. Your choices matter. Your family deserves better. And together, through thousands of improved homes, we can improve our society.

Because when Nigerian homes thrive, Nigeria thrives.
